## Full description

```
Turn your phone into a remote for Android TV and Google TV. It works over your home Wi-Fi with the same pairing as Google's own remote, so there's nothing to install on the TV.

AIR MOUSE
Point your phone at the TV and a pointer follows. Tap the pad to click and swipe to scroll. It's made for apps and browsers that don't work well with arrow keys. The phone connects to the TV as a Bluetooth mouse.
• Recenter brings a lost pointer back to the middle of the screen
• Pointing pauses when you put the phone down
• Vol + clicks and Vol − goes Back while you point (you can change this)
• Turn Air pointing off to use the pad like a laptop touchpad
• Adjust sensitivity and smoothing, or calibrate a pointer that drifts

A FULL REMOTE ON ONE SCREEN
• A big D-pad with OK, or a touchpad if you prefer swiping
• Back, Home, power, mute, volume and media keys, with no scrolling
• Hold any key to repeat it or for a long press
• The phone's volume buttons change the TV's volume

TYPE WITH YOUR PHONE'S KEYBOARD
When a search box on the TV asks for text, a typing sheet opens on your phone. It shows what the TV actually received, so you know your text got there. For YouTube, a button opens the results for your search on the TV.

FINDS YOUR TV BY ITSELF
The app looks for TVs on your Wi-Fi, including networks that hide them. You can also add a TV by its IP address. Pair once with the code shown on the TV, and the app reconnects to the last TV when you open it.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account, no ads, no analytics. The app talks only to the TV you pick, on your own network. It remembers the TV's certificate and refuses a different device answering at the same address.

REQUIREMENTS
• An Android TV or Google TV on the same Wi-Fi network as your phone
• Android 7.0 or later for the remote
• For the air mouse: Android 9 or later, Bluetooth, and a phone whose maker left the Bluetooth mouse feature turned on. The app checks this and only offers the air mouse on phones that can do it. A gyroscope is needed for pointing. Without one, the pad works as a touchpad.

```
